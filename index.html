THE COLLECTION / 01

Sternstunde.

By Zhichu (稚楚)

Synopsis

When they first met, Qin Yiyu saw Nan Yi standing outside his front door, his expression cold and a liquor bottle clutched in his hand. Naturally, he assumed the guy had come to collect a debt.

Instead, Nan Yi said:

"I'm putting together a band. We're entering a competition, and we're missing a lead vocalist who can play guitar."

Qin Yiyu thought: Great. Somehow, this is even worse than debt collectors.

But in the end, he agreed. After all, he couldn't bear to let such a talented bassist slip through his fingers.

When the competition finally arrived, nobody had much faith in the newly formed band. Even the staff were gossiping backstage:

"Sure, the bassist is handsome, but you can't win with a pretty face. I've heard the drummer has such a bad temper he nearly got into a fight onstage. The keyboardist apparently used to work a desk job at an investment bank before getting fired. And then there's the vocalist: popular, sure, but apparently a terrible person who was kicked out of his previous band. With a makeshift group like this, do they really stand a chance of winning?"

Unfortunately for them, all four band members were standing right behind them.

Yan Ji: "First of all, let's get a few things straight. I quit voluntarily. And it was an investment bank, not some ordinary bank..."

Chi Zhiyang: "What the hell are you even saying?! Since when do I have a bad temper?!"

Qin Yiyu: "Blah, blah, blah. I stopped listening after the first four words. Someone as perfect as me can only have two kinds of haters: people secretly in love with me, and people with absolutely zero taste. Pathetic."
