THE BACK-COVER NOTE

About the story

When they first met, Qin Yiyu saw Nan Yi standing outside his front door, his expression cold and a liquor bottle clutched in his hand. Naturally, he assumed the guy had come to collect a debt.

Instead, Nan Yi said:

“I'm putting together a band. We're entering a competition, and we're missing a lead vocalist who can play guitar.”

Qin Yiyu thought: Great. Somehow, this is even worse than debt collectors.

But in the end, he agreed. After all, he couldn't bear to let such a talented bassist slip through his fingers.

When the competition finally arrived, nobody had much faith in the newly formed band. Even the staff were gossiping backstage — unfortunately for them, all four band members were standing right behind them.

Synopsis adapted for this translation project.

A NOTE FROM THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGES THE TRANSLATOR

A READER WITH TOO MANY OPEN TABS

So, who's
Sivren?

READER
FIRST
✳

Hi, I'm Sivren.

I'm a reader with a slightly inconvenient habit: getting attached to webnovels that have absolutely no decent English translation.

You know how it goes. You find a story that looks incredible, get excited, search for an English translation, and discover that your options are either machine translations, translations that are barely readable, or absolutely nothing.

And sometimes, there isn't even a translation to complain about.

That's pretty much how I ended up here.

I started translating because I wanted to read these stories. Not someday, not after someone else picked them up, and certainly not through a machine translation that makes me question whether I've understood anything correctly.

So I'm doing it myself.

Which means, yes, I'm translating and reading at the same time. I'm figuring out the story as I go, just like you will. Consider us fellow readers, except one of us has a dictionary open in another tab and is probably fighting with a particularly stubborn sentence.

marginalia. is where I keep these translations. It's a personal project, a little corner for stories that deserve an English-speaking audience, and hopefully a place where other readers can finally enjoy the novels they've been searching for.

If you're here because you couldn't find a readable translation either, well, welcome. You're in the right place.

— Sivren
